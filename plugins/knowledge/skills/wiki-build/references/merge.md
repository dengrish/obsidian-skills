# Merge Logic — integration, frontmatter, source-no-op merges, conflicts

Scope: collisions and new-source merges; corrections or deepening from already-cited sources alone are wiki-lint's, under its [source-backed corrections](../../wiki-lint/references/source-backed-corrections.md) protocol.

## Collision decisions

`find_collisions.py` checks filenames **and aliases**, plus the common names an Organism's description or opener binds (adjudicate only), then candidates against each other:

| Probe | Comparison | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (a) Basic slug equality | Candidate slug as-is | Merge only with one existing owner |
| (b) µ-variant | Microsign `µ` and Greek mu `μ` equivalents | Merge only with one existing owner |
| (c) Singular/plural | Regular and irregular inflections | Adjudicate |
| (d) Hyphenation-collapse | Hyphens stripped on both sides | Adjudicate |
| (e) Word-order-permutation | Singularized tokens, sorted | Adjudicate |
| (f) Stem-morphology | Gerund/noun and other morphological pairs | Adjudicate |
| (g) Token-superset | One stemmed token set extends the other by at most two tokens | Adjudicate |

Multiple owners for (a) or (b) also need adjudication; never choose by index order. The other probes flag possible duplicates, not identity. A confirmed duplicate goes to one canonical entry, titled by the [title rule](writing.md#title) and its acronym-or-full-form choice, with a valid alternate slug as an alias; this never authorizes wiki-build to merge, delete or rename pre-existing files: report the duplicate or rename, which wiki-lint's ordinary run applies once its evidence holds. Distinct but confusable entities follow [same-surface-form handling](special-titles.md#same-surface-form-different-technique).

An empty wiki still needs candidate-to-candidate checks. Index problems that can hide ownership (unreadable, unsafe or unparsed entries, including an unclosed or malformed frontmatter fence, and malformed titles or aliases) keep unmatched candidates, and exact matches that rest on an alias alone, at `adjudicate`: never create from apparent absence, and report every index problem. A readable plain note without frontmatter claims no title or alias: report it, but it blocks nothing. Its own filename is still occupied and is never a merge destination.

## Merge Logic

### Integration principle

Integrate the active source into **one coherent explanation organized by the entity**, not by source chronology. Before rewriting, apply the [source-no-op test](#source-no-op-merges). For a regular merge:

- **Preservation protects claims, not arrangement.** Keep every substantive existing claim, the meaning of each equation, every exhibit and the primary card unless inspected evidence corrects it under [conflict handling](#conflict-handling); [flashcards on merge](#flashcards-on-merge) removes any other card. Uncited hand edits, including their deliberate structure, are merge input too; preserve and report an unresolved conflict rather than discarding one. Otherwise paragraph order, wording, notation and which display leads are not protected.
- **Rebuild in teaching order.** Weave each new fact where it advances a paragraph's idea, and reorder, split or rewrite paragraphs into the [learner's order](writing.md#prose-principles) under the [flow sweep](writing.md#editorial-reread). When the merged material makes a more general definition central, restructure: the general definition first, in vault notation; then the specialized forms as cases; then derived results. Restate a carried-over equation in the unified notation when it is the same quantity. Change the opener only for a meaningfully better main claim, such as that more general definition.
- **Restructuring inside this entry is integration, not a refactor.** Reapply [atomic scope](writing.md#body-structure): create and link a distinct entity the new source substantively teaches, but moving pre-existing off-scope content to another entry, or deleting it, belongs to wiki-lint, whose ordinary run consolidates a duplicated explanation into its owner and splits an entry that defines several durable subjects; report the proposed move or split instead. The one exception is the [ownership handoff](review.md#overlapownership-audit-this-runs-entries-and-their-relevant-neighbors). There is no size target, and an optional example or figure does not earn a place merely by being available.
- Link provenance follows [CONVENTIONS §9](../../../shared/CONVENTIONS.md#9-ownership-split-for-linking): link a target only when the active source introduces it or contributes a substantive relationship to it. Rewording a carried-over claim never restores a previously pruned link; genuine new source support may, and is reported.
- For `Software`, reapply the [API substance gate](api-surface.md#software-explain-the-artifact-not-its-reference-manual).

**Check the completed body.** Resolve accidental concatenation into one body: duplicate openers or bolded title introductions, duplicated mechanism explanations, a sense or fact stated in two places (a secondary sense in both the opener and the last paragraph), stray YAML keys or digits, and unexpected `---` fences (the separator before `## Flashcards` is legitimate). Then re-read every carried-over sentence and symbol binding against the merged opener's scope: confirm each carried-over claim survives, remove carried-over hedges (the [hedge sweep](writing.md#editorial-reread)), defensive clarifications and repeated conventions that [principle 3](writing.md#prose-principles) excludes (never from an uncited hand edit), and report each removal.

### Frontmatter and Related footer

[Schema](../../../shared/CONVENTIONS.md#2-frontmatter-schemas) and [source-reference rules](../../../shared/CONVENTIONS.md#7-source-references) govern encoding, [writing fields](writing.md#1-frontmatter-fields) meaning. Apply these rules only when a value actually changes:

1. **`sources:`** Append a missing full citation, deduplicated, at the entity's introducing physical PDF page (Markdown unanchored); report a rerun's anchor drift. Replace a Markdown summary with its PDF only after decoded provenance confirms the pair (a *Review-pass fix*); preserve independent clippings and unresolved references, reporting uncertainty. A document is cited in one representation: never append a chapter beside its whole-book PDF ([books and chapters](source-intake.md#books-and-chapters)) or a clipping beside its same-URL twin ([prior coverage](source-intake.md#check-prior-coverage)); keep the existing citation and report the decision. An entry that cites a split book's chapters cites a whole-book source's contribution as the chapter PDF that holds it, at the introducing passage's physical page in that chapter; when no chapter holds that page, keep the existing citation and report the decision. Keep every online page's [URL item](../../../shared/CONVENTIONS.md#7-source-references) exactly and in place and never add one, except that an active source proven to be the same document replaces it: a clipping, when `python3 '<plugin>/skills/clipping-clean/scripts/dedup_index.py' '<vault>/Articles' --url '<URL item>'` returns a `checked` row whose `matches` includes that clipping's path, or a PDF whose printed DOI or arXiv identifier is the one the URL names. Report the replacement as a *Review-pass fix*; any other result keeps both items and reports the pair under *Notes for the user*.
2. **`updated:`** Today's local date when the entry actually changes, metadata and independent QC included; a byte-unchanged result keeps it. `created:` never changes.
3. **`aliases:`** Append only valid same-entity aliases from the new source, deduplicated in slug form. Preserve a pre-existing semantic-invalid alias and report its evidence and likely owner; only wiki-lint removes it, through its [alias-removal protocol](../../wiki-lint/references/refactors.md#remove-a-semantic-invalid-alias), though within-list duplicates may be cleaned up.
4. **`tags:`** Keep exactly one supported home. Correct a clearly wrong home, multiple homes, or a blank or empty list from the entity's meaning (`"#misc"` only when nothing fits); preserve and report ambiguous or malformed metadata; report old and new groups for wiki-lint's hierarchy refresh.
5. **`parents:` and legacy `importance:`** Preserve exactly as found: never populate parents, migrate legacy importance, or strip it during a body rewrite.
6. **`description:`** Rewrite only for a meaningfully more accurate, informative or grammatically clear result, or to fix a concrete [description defect](writing.md#description); rephrasing alone is insufficient, and a source-no-op permits defect repairs only.
7. **Related footer:** Keep existing links; add only substantive relationships the active source introduces, never from a carried-over bare mention or on a source-no-op. Targeted QC may canonicalize a target or label or remove a proven invalid link; weak-link pruning is wiki-lint's. Report growth past roughly 12 links rather than pruning ([footer guidance](writing.md#the-related-footer)).
8. **Keys outside the schema:** preserve Obsidian-owned and other unexpected keys exactly and in place under [§2a](../../../shared/CONVENTIONS.md#2a-wiki-entry--wikimd), and list them under *Notes for the user*.
9. **`issues:`** The user's issue inbox ([CONVENTIONS §2d](../../../shared/CONVENTIONS.md#2d-issues--the-users-issue-inbox)). Preserve an existing value byte-for-byte, blank or not; never act on, edit or clear its text, which wiki-lint resolves. `lint_entry.py` reports user text as `2-user-issues` and a malformed value as `2-issues-malformed`, both report-only: preserve and report them, never re-quote or repair them. When the key is missing, insert `issues: ""` directly after `read:` (after the last schema key that precedes it when `read:` is absent too), a format fix.

### Exhibits and headings

**Images and tables.** Preserve existing images, recreated tables and captions with their motivating prose; for one that no longer fits, propose consolidation or removal, never deletion or a justifying tangent. Replace an image only with a strictly clearer or more complete depiction of the **same specific aspect**, not merely the same subject, and a table only with **the same data** presented more completely or correctly; different datasets never subsume each other. Name both exhibits and the benefit in the report; keep the underlying files.

**Equations** follow [equations on merge](equations.md#4-normalization--the-sources-symbols-do-not-survive-contact); a simple verbal concept gets no forced mathematics.

**Headings** are narrative organization, not preserved exhibits. Add, remove, rename, or reorder `##` headings when the integrated explanation warrants it under [Body structure](writing.md#body-structure), never merely because of length. A new-source subsection with a distinct supported identity becomes its own candidate; moving pre-existing content to another entry stays wiki-lint's refactor.

### Flashcards on merge

Before the item-19 QC below, read the [card format](flashcards-and-emphasis.md#4-flashcards); before changing a card, also read wiki-lint's [improving the card](../../wiki-lint/references/flashcards.md#improving-the-card) for its convergence and attachment rules; a merge changes line 1 only as below.

- **Every merge, including source-no-ops:** run item-19 QC on the primary card. Restore a missing card, except on a discipline root, from the entry's established main claim with `??`; repair current-rule defects in place. Bring card math under [line-1 equation coverage](flashcards-and-emphasis.md#line-1-equation-coverage) for the same tested claim, preserving the answer line and attachments; that repair never invents an equation or changes the learning objective.
- **Regular merges, primary definition:** if integration substantively changes the main claim, re-derive line 1 and replace the old one when the new line is a clearer, more precise, more concise or fairer definition of the new main claim. A line 1 that already states the new main claim as the card guide asks stays byte-for-byte; never reword it for variety. If inspected evidence corrects a claim and its card now contradicts the body, correct the card in place rather than removing and re-adding it.
- **Primary answer:** recompute its content when the canonical title, qualifying alias counterpart, or the opener's direct binding changes; apply the answer contract, preserving the separator and attachments.
- **No second card:** a merge never adds a card. A key result the source adds belongs in the body, and a second source-supported entity earns its own entry.
- **Extra cards, on every merge, including source-no-ops:** remove every card other than the primary card, under the [card set](flashcards-and-emphasis.md#card-set), and quote each verbatim, attachments included, in *Notes for the user*. When a removed card tested a distinct real entity a cited source supports, name that entity there as a missing-entry candidate with the [missing-entry routes](../../../shared/CONVENTIONS.md#9-ownership-split-for-linking), as wiki-lint's [card set](../../wiki-lint/references/flashcards.md#card-set) does; it is not logged. When no card is a definition card, rewrite the first card into one, keeping its attachments, and remove the rest. A removal advances `updated:` and keeps `read:`. Keep, and report under [step 7.2](../SKILL.md#7-review-and-report), an extra card whose block ID another note links or embeds; wiki-lint retargets those references and removes it.

Every pre-existing separator and recognized scheduling or block-ID attachment on the kept card stays byte-for-byte and in place, even when a content line changes, apart from the [`??` restoration](flashcards-and-emphasis.md#line-2-the-separator). A conforming card stays unchanged. Content after line 3 that is not a recognized attachment, such as a schedule a blank line separates from the card, stays byte-for-byte and is reported under *Notes for the user*, never removed, moved or reattached, unless it holds [card syntax](flashcards-and-emphasis.md#card-set), which makes it an extra card.

### The `read:` reset

Apply the shared [review-checkbox contract](../../../shared/CONVENTIONS.md#2c-read--the-users-review-checkbox). This section is the shared **body-change rule**, which wiki-lint's content repair (Task 1b), source-backed corrections and refactors also follow. Never infer missing, null, or unknown user state. For a known value, set `read: false` **only when adding or rewriting unread explanatory body content**, whether from the active source or independent step-7 QC: prose, deepening, an image or recreated table, a newly typeset or inserted equation, a Person/Event date, a rewritten explanation, or an explanation moved into its owner entry. Two wiki-lint exceptions, which a builder never applies, set `read: false` from any prior state, missing or unknown included, with or without a body change: its [user-issue override](../../wiki-lint/SKILL.md#dates), when a run resolves at least one of an entry's user issues ([CONVENTIONS §2d](../../../shared/CONVENTIONS.md#2d-issues--the-users-issue-inbox)); and the surviving entry of its [merge](../../wiki-lint/references/refactors.md#build-the-refactored-entries), which holds combined content the user has not read in that form.

Metadata/alias/source appends, alias removals, added Related-footer links, description/card/footer/tag repairs, extra-card removals, content removal (trims, hedge removals, consolidation and ownership-handoff trims), notation renames, retitles, link-only changes, an added acronym or full-form parenthetical, naming and linking a contrast in an existing sentence, and format-only fixes do not reset it. `updated:` still follows any actual change, a title change included, apart from the rewritten-inbound-link and issue-blanking exceptions in wiki-lint's [Dates](../../wiki-lint/SKILL.md#dates). A new entry is `read: false`. When the unread-content judgment is otherwise genuinely close, preserve `read:` and report the decision. Name every reset and every close no-reset call in the run report.

### Source-no-op merges

A candidate must first pass step 2's substance and, for a secondary source, durability filters. After [example](writing.md#prose-principles), [figure](media.md#selection) and [table](media.md#tables) selection, compare **every selected source contribution** with the existing entry: a fact is covered when present verbatim or as a clear paraphrase, a figure by the same `Sources/Images/` filename, a table by the same recreated data, an equation by the same quantity's definition (different notation alone is not new), and for `Software` an interface or design idea rather than identifier spelling. If all are covered, the entry is **source-no-op-merged**; one new selected fact, relationship, image, table or equation makes the merge regular, while a documented decision to skip an optional example or figure is not net-new. Explicit reruns still need this comparison.

A source-no-op skips source-driven body, description, card and Related-footer rewriting, leaving conforming content byte-for-byte unchanged. It still applies the metadata rules, including the active source's missing citation, and **all step-7 QC and audits**, making only their authorized repairs: a missing Person/Event date under [rare-types](rare-types.md#dates-in-the-opener-person-and-event), an equation warranted by carried-over prose, removal of a navigation-only cue, a `noncanonical` link rewrite, a description or card defect, or an extra card's removal.

Classification concerns the source's contribution, not the final bytes: an independent QC equation leaves the source a no-op while advancing `updated:` and resetting a known `read:` value, whereas an equation the active source contributes makes the merge regular. Report them under the [run report](review.md#run-report)'s source-no-op bullet, never describing a QC-changing run as leaving the wiki unchanged.

### Net-new fact test

Compare meaning before wording: "scales pairwise gradients by the NDCG change from swapping items" adds nothing to an entry already weighting pairs by that change; a new source-supported relationship to LambdaLoss is net-new. Integrate a net-new fact under the [integration principle](#integration-principle), never as a source-shaped addendum.

### Conflict handling

Resolve a contradiction only from the active source and the existing entry's cited sources you can inspect. A direction, sign or relationship that follows by direct derivation from a formula the entry or one of those sources states also settles it, and the derivation becomes the entry's reason. A claim resting only on a cited URL, which the builder does not fetch, is unresolved evidence to preserve and log for wiki-lint, whose content repair reads that page and standard references. Methods, scope, date and authority weigh evidence only when those sources establish them, and memory, uncited background knowledge or assumed consensus never settle it.

- **Existing claim supported, new contradiction unsupported:** preserve the existing claim and omit the contradictory clause; integrate unrelated supported contributions and record a qualifying source in `sources:`.
- **Existing claim disproved by inspected evidence:** correct it in place.
- **Evidence partial or unresolved:** synthesize only what the sources jointly support; otherwise preserve the existing claim, omit the contradictory clause, and report both claims with source pages, logging the conflict in `Reviews/wiki-notes-suggestions.md` under the `[conflicting-claims]` item, adding the instance to its Evidence.

Report every correction or unresolved conflict with its evidence; write the resolved subject as ordinary prose under [prose principle 5](writing.md#prose-principles), not a source-by-source transcript.

**Contested-topic exemption.** When legitimately coexisting views (philosophical, historical or religious positions) make the disagreement part of the subject, present source-supported views in parallel with light attribution, in fresh entries and merges alike; the (a)–(d) source-meta prohibitions of [principle 5](writing.md#prose-principles) otherwise apply. Scientific and established-versus-fringe disputes use the evidence rule above, stating consensus or fringe status only when inspected sources establish it.

**Neighbor conflicts.** A linked neighbor that states the same defining sense, relationship, direction or number differently ([contradiction check](writing.md#editorial-reread)) is checked against the same evidence: correct this run's side when it is wrong. When the evidence settles neither side, this run's prose states only what the evidence jointly supports and omits the contested number, direction or sense, as a merge does above. When the active source disproves the neighbor's claim and passes step 2 for the neighbor's entity, correct the neighbor as a merge ([scope](../SKILL.md#scope-and-files)). Otherwise leave the neighbor unchanged, report the conflict under *Notes for the user*, and log it in `Reviews/wiki-notes-suggestions.md` under the `[conflicting-claims]` item, adding the instance (both passages and the evidence) to its Evidence; wiki-lint's ordinary run resolves it.
