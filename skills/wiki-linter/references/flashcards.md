# Flashcards — when a card may be rewritten (Task 1, item 19)

**Read this whenever an entry has a `## Flashcards` section you are about to touch** — i.e. on any `item19` finding, and on every full entry when Task 1 runs a definition review. It is the judgment layer on top of item 19's mechanical floor (`references/qc-items.md`); the two bars below decide whether a card may be rewritten at all.

### Flashcard definition review (item 19)

The leak scan — item 19's mechanical floor, spelled out in `references/qc-items.md` — is only the *floor* of item 19. Because wiki-builder writes `??` cards, **each card is two-sided** — drilled both definition→term and term→definition (the line-4 metadata carries one schedule per side) — so the definition (line 1) has to read clearly in **both** directions:

- **Forward (definition → term): uniquely identifying.** Given the definition, exactly one term should fit. The substring leak check only guarantees the answer isn't *literally* in the prompt; a definition can leak nothing yet still be ambiguous. Judge whether any *other* entity could answer to it — and use the linter's whole-vault vantage to do what wiki-builder structurally cannot (it sees one source at a time): **compare the definition against every other entry's definition and scope.** If it also fits a sibling — `k-means` and `k-medoids` both reading "a clustering algorithm that partitions data into k groups" — it fails; add the distinguishing detail (for `k-means`, that the centers are coordinate means). On a large vault an exhaustive all-pairs comparison isn't realistic — surface the collisions you notice (siblings, same-family methods, near-synonyms) rather than treating this as a guaranteed every-pair scan; like the rest of item 19's ceiling, it's judgment, not a mechanical floor.
- **Backward (term → definition): the canonical answer.** Given the term, the definition must be the answer a learner is fairly expected to recall — the entity's **main defining claim** (the same scope as the body's opening sentence), not an arbitrary true fact about it. "An architecture introduced in 2017" is true of the Transformer but backward-unfair (and not self-contained); the fair answer names the essential property — self-attention replacing recurrence.
- **Succinct.** One sentence, as short as unambiguity allows; lengthen only as far as needed to rule out other candidates. (Judgment, not mechanized — sentence-counting trips on abbreviations, decimals, and LaTeX, so it stays off the deterministic floor.)

**The rewrite bar depends on whether the card has been studied.** A card's line 4 (`<!--SR:…-->`) is the Obsidian Spaced Repetition plugin's schedule, attached to that card *by position*. Because it is positional it survives a line-1 edit — but that is exactly the hazard: the schedule stays attached while the thing being tested changes underneath it, so a card the learner has rated over months silently starts prompting for something they never rated. Review history is the load-bearing asset; a sharper phrasing is not worth re-pointing it. So there are two bars, and **wiki-builder's high bar governs the studied case**:

- **Card with review history (a line 4+ is present) — high bar.** Replace line 1 **only when the new definition is meaningfully more accurate or unambiguous** than the existing one (wiki-builder's *Flashcards on merge* threshold — in `wiki-builder/references/merge.md` — the same one it applies to `description:`). A genuine defect qualifies: the definition leaks the answer, is factually wrong, is ambiguous against a sibling entity, or is backward-unfair (not the entity's main defining claim). **Cosmetic improvement does not:** a verbose-but-correct sentence, a phrasing that is merely sharper, a stylistic preference. Leave those alone and, if the shortfall is real but sub-threshold, note it in `wiki-notes-suggestions.md` rather than rewriting.
- **Fresh card (no line 4 — never studied) — low bar.** Nothing is attached to it yet, so there is no continuity to protect. Fix any genuine shortfall down to the minor ones (verbosity, a borderline ambiguity, a phrasing that is actually sharper).

A **structural** fix — one the mechanical floor demands, such as stripping wikilinks/bold/backticks from line 1, or removing a literal answer leak — is applied to **every** card regardless of history: those are violations of wiki-builder's schema, not stylistic preferences, and leaving them in place is not an option.

- **Churn-avoidance still applies, at either bar.** Even the fresh-card bar fixes *real* flaws only; it does **not** reword for its own sake. A definition that already reads as one succinct, uniquely-identifying, backward-fair sentence is **left byte-for-byte** — don't churn a sound card each run (this also keeps the pass idempotent).
- **Two hard invariants that never relax** (these are not part of the threshold): preserve line 4 **verbatim** — never edit, reformat, or drop the `<!--SR:…-->` metadata — and **never reorder cards**; both keep each schedule correctly attached even as line 1 changes. wiki-linter does content QC of the definition only; the Spaced Repetition plugin still owns line 4 and scheduling, untouched and unaudited.

The mechanical scan now covers **every** card in the section (structure, line-2 form, line-1 markup, per-card answer-leak, line-3 plainness); this bidirectional-clarity review is the judgment layer on top of it, also applied to every card.

**Multiple cards.** wiki-builder's rule is **exactly one card per entry** (the main claim). A section holding more than one card is an item-19 violation with a determinate fix (`references/qc-items.md` item 19): keep the card whose line 3 matches the complete primary-answer contract — canonical `title:` verbatim, or the base term for a parenthetical-disambiguated title, plus any opener-established counterpart whose slug is present in `aliases:`. The qualifying classes are an acronym/full-form pair, a direct `(short for *…*)` expansion, and a direct italic scientific abbreviation such as `*E. coli*`; a synonym's abbreviation remains excluded. Delete the rest, preserve the kept card's line 4+ verbatim, and quote every removed card verbatim in the report. When a removed card's term is a real entity a cited source supports, propose a **split** — a new entry for that term — in the report; don't create the entry yourself (refactoring is out of scope, the same way sprawling entries are flagged). The definition review below applies to the one kept card.

**Using LaTeX in a rewrite.** When the linter does rewrite a line-1 definition (per the review above), it should reach for symbolic form without hesitation where a symbol or equation sharpens the definition — a metric's defining equation, a quantity's notation, a Greek letter for a hyperparameter — because the symbolic form is often more uniquely identifying than a verbal paraphrase, which is exactly what an answer-key definition needs. Inline `$…$` only — line 1 never carries a `$$…$$` display block, which would break the card's three-contiguous-line structure (`wiki-builder/references/equations.md` §2: captions and flashcard line 1 allow inline math only); the RHS-only LHS-strip where an equation's left side would leak the term; never wikilinks/bold/italic/backticks on line 1.
