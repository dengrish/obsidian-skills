# Flashcards — reviewing and improving the card (item 19)

**Read this whenever a card may be added, changed or removed**: on any `item19` finding, and on every entry when Task 1 runs a definition review. It is the judgment layer on top of item 19's mechanical floor ([scanner item keys](scanner.md#item-keys-in-problems)). The card format and card set belong to wiki-build's [card guide](../../wiki-build/references/flashcards-and-emphasis.md#4-flashcards).

## Flashcard definition review (item 19)

The definition card is drilled in both directions, so its line 1 must read clearly both ways:

- **Forward (cue → term): uniquely identifying without rebuilding the answer.** The mechanical floor catches literal title and alias leaks across case, whitespace and punctuation variants. Review semantic ones too: an expanded acronym ("machine learning operations practice" for `MLOps`), all title words reordered, or a synonym swapped into each word of the title or an alias, unless the name is itself the definition (Mean absolute error). A cue can also leak nothing yet fit a rival. Compare it with the entry's `card_rivals` from the scan (Related targets and entries sharing a non-root parent), plus aliases, near-synonymous titles, members of its stated family and, as in the builder's [sibling check](../../wiki-build/references/flashcards-and-emphasis.md#line-1-the-cue), the terms a learner would most likely confuse with it, entries or not, asking of each: could that term answer this cue? A yes is an ambiguity defect; add the essential property that sets the entity apart (for `k-means`, that each center is the mean of its points). Record which rival classes were checked; never claim an exhaustive comparison.
- **Backward (term → cue): the canonical answer.** Given the term, line 1 must be what a learner is fairly expected to recall: the entity's **main defining claim**, stated as its kind plus its essential property. "An architecture introduced in 2017" is true of the Transformer but backward-unfair; the fair answer names self-attention replacing recurrence. When the body defines the subject with an equation, the cue keeps every defining operation: a card for an Lp norm that omits the p-th root defines a different quantity.
- **Succinct.** One sentence of about 20 words outside math, lengthened only as far as ruling out rivals needs. The shared floor counts sentences conservatively; judge any boundary it cannot prove. `item19/brevity-candidate` is a review candidate, never an order.

**Line-1 math.** Check any card math against the [line-1 equation rule](../../wiki-build/references/flashcards-and-emphasis.md#line-1-equation-coverage), even when the scanner is silent; its line-1 `item12/boilerplate-candidate` lists only common boilerplate shapes. When the tested concept is unchanged, bring card math under that rule: shorten the math, or replace a long formula with its verbal core while the body keeps the formula. When the body lacks the card's formula, Task 1b adds it to the body from the cited source or accurate background under [item 12](qc-items.md#12-equations-images-and-tables), or replaces the card math with its verbal core when the formula fails that item's usefulness test. Lint never invents body math.

## Improving the card

Lint may revise line 1, or rewrite it completely, whenever the result is clearer, more precise, more concise or a fairer definition under the [card guide](../../wiki-build/references/flashcards-and-emphasis.md#line-1-the-cue): better phrasing, sharper discrimination from rivals, a plainer statement of the kind and property. Review history never limits rewording. Fix every item-19 floor finding on the card too, such as forbidden markup, a literal leak or a line 3 missing the [counterpart](../../wiki-build/references/flashcards-and-emphasis.md#line-3-the-answer) the opener binds and `aliases:` lists. Report each card edit with its old and new line 1. A [user issue](../SKILL.md#user-issues) about the card is a request for this review, and the review resolves it. One whose fix changes the tested claim goes to Task 1b under *Dates* below.

- **Convergence.** A card already as clear, precise and concise as the guide asks stays byte-for-byte: never reword for variety, so a rerun on unchanged evidence changes nothing.
- **Separator.** A user-disabled `!!` is the user's choice and stays. A line 2 other than `??` or `!!`, such as a card simplified to `?`, [regains `??`](../../wiki-build/references/flashcards-and-emphasis.md#line-2-the-separator).
- **Attachments.** Every recognized schedule, exact metadata callout and trailing block ID on the kept card stays **byte-for-byte and in place**, even when line 1 is rewritten; never move an attachment between cards. A block ID is an inbound-link anchor; answer-line plainness never authorizes deleting it. Other visible content after the three content lines, including a blank-line-separated `<!--SR:` block, is malformed but may be user text or a detached schedule: preserve it byte-for-byte, never delete, move or reattach it, and report it under *Notes for the user*, unless it holds [card syntax](../../wiki-build/references/flashcards-and-emphasis.md#card-set), which makes it an extra card under the [card set](#card-set). The Spaced Repetition plugin owns its schedules, untouched and unaudited.
- **Dates.** A Task 1 card edit, including one that resolves a user issue, keeps `created:` and `updated:`; `read:` changes only under the [user-issue override](../SKILL.md#dates). When a [Task 1b](../SKILL.md#task-1b--content-repair) repair corrects the claim a card tests, such as a wrong fact, a [wrong sense](qc-items.md#7-description) or a variant in place of the prototype, it judges the card against the same evidence as the description and opener and rewrites it in the same edit, under Task 1b's dates. A repair that leaves the tested claim true, such as deepening, leaves the card alone.

A missing card has nothing to preserve: restore it from the entry's established main claim, with `??`, unless the entry is a [discipline root](hierarchy.md#establish-discipline-roots), which needs none.

## Card set

An entry holds one definition card, the [card](../../wiki-build/references/flashcards-and-emphasis.md#card-set) that tests its main claim; a discipline root may have none. Never add a second card. With several cards, the definition card is the first whose line 3 meets the [answer contract](../../wiki-build/references/flashcards-and-emphasis.md#line-3-the-answer), ignoring attachments, else its one near miss.

Lint keeps the definition card and removes every other card, whatever its separator, with its recognized attachments; no request is needed. When a removed card carries a block ID, rewrite each link or embed to it in vault Markdown as a plain link to the entry, or in a merge to the survivor, keeping any label, under the [refactor rewrite scope](refactors.md#establish-evidence-and-complete-scope), and list it with the removed card in the report. Where that scope retains an old target, as for an `Investments/` note, keep the card and report it. When no card is a definition card, rewrite the first card into one under [Improving the card](#improving-the-card), keeping its attachments, and remove the rest. Content after a removed card that is not a recognized attachment stays, under the attachment rule above. Quote each removed card verbatim, attachments included, in the report; when it tested a distinct real entity a cited source supports, name that entity as a missing-entry candidate. A removal advances `updated:` and keeps `read:`.
