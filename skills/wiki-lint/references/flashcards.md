# Flashcards — when a card may be rewritten (Task 1, item 19)

**Read this whenever a card may be added or changed**: on any `item19` finding, and on every entry when Task 1 runs a definition review. It is the judgment layer on top of item 19's mechanical floor ([scanner item keys](scanner.md#item-keys-in-problems)). The card format and card set belong to wiki-build's [card guide](../../wiki-build/references/flashcards-and-emphasis.md#4-flashcards).

## Flashcard definition review (item 19)

The primary `??` card is drilled in both directions, so its line 1 must read clearly both ways:

- **Forward (cue → term): uniquely identifying without rebuilding the answer.** The mechanical floor catches literal title and alias leaks across case, whitespace and punctuation variants. Review semantic ones too: an expanded acronym ("machine learning operations practice" for `MLOps`), all title words reordered, or a synonym swapped into each word of the title or an alias, unless the name is itself the definition (Mean absolute error). A cue can also leak nothing yet fit a rival. Compare it with the entry's `card_rivals` from the scan (Related targets and entries sharing a non-root parent), plus aliases, near-synonymous titles and members of its stated family, asking of each: could that term answer this cue? A yes is an ambiguity defect; add the essential property that sets the entity apart (for `k-means`, that each center is the mean of its points). Record which rival classes you checked; never claim an exhaustive comparison.
- **Backward (term → cue): the canonical answer.** Given the term, line 1 must be what a learner is fairly expected to recall: for the primary card, the entity's **main defining claim**, stated as its kind plus its essential property. "An architecture introduced in 2017" is true of the Transformer but backward-unfair; the fair answer names self-attention replacing recurrence. When the body defines the subject with an equation, the cue keeps every defining operation: a card for an Lp norm that omits the p-th root defines a different quantity.
- **Succinct.** One sentence of about 20 words outside math, lengthened only as far as ruling out rivals needs. The shared floor counts sentences conservatively; judge any boundary it cannot prove. `item19/brevity-candidate` is a review candidate, never an order.

**Line-1 math.** Check any card math against the [line-1 equation rule](../../wiki-build/references/flashcards-and-emphasis.md#line-1-equation-coverage), even when the scanner is silent; its line-1 `item12/boilerplate-candidate` lists only common boilerplate shapes. Bringing card math under that rule is a targeted current-rule repair at either bar when the tested concept is unchanged: shorten the math, or replace a long formula with its verbal core while the body keeps the formula. When the body lacks the card's formula, preserve the card and report the missing support. Routine lint never invents body math. Preserve the separator, the answer line and every attachment; ordinary lint also preserves `created:`, `updated:` and `read:`. Report the repair; the maintenance request is sufficient authorization.

## Card freshness and the rewrite bars

A recognized attachment proves that a card carries review state; its absence alone proves nothing, because the plugin may store schedules outside the note. A card is **known fresh** when this run created or restored it, or when it carries no recognized attachment and the scan's `spaced_repetition.schedules_outside_notes` is `false`. Review history is the asset these bars protect: a schedule stays attached while a line-1 edit changes what is tested.

- **High bar, for every card not known fresh.** Beyond line-1 math and structural repairs, replace line 1 **only for a genuine defect**: it leaks or rebuilds the answer, is factually wrong, also fits a rival, is backward-unfair, names no kind (it fails the `<term> is` test), names a variant instead of the [prototype](../../wiki-build/references/writing.md#prose-principles), discriminates by trivia (a measurement, date, count, etymology or anecdote), or carries a hedge, exception or second clause the definition does not need. A verbose-but-correct or merely sharper sentence is cosmetic: leave it, and log a real sub-threshold shortfall in `Reviews/wiki-notes-suggestions.md`.
- **Low bar, for a known-fresh card.** Fix any genuine shortfall, down to verbosity, borderline ambiguity or a meaningfully sharper phrasing.

A **structural** fix that the mechanical floor demands (forbidden markup, a math delimiter, a literal leak, or a line 2 other than `??` or `!!`, such as a card simplified to `?`, which [regains `??`](../../wiki-build/references/flashcards-and-emphasis.md#line-2-the-separator)) applies to the primary card regardless of history.

- **Churn-avoidance applies at either bar.** A card that already meets the rules stays byte-for-byte; never reword for its own sake, so a rerun changes nothing.
- **Two invariants never relax:** preserve every recognized schedule, exact metadata callout and trailing block ID on every pre-existing card **byte-for-byte and in place**, and never move an attachment between cards. A block ID is an inbound-link anchor; answer-line plainness never authorizes deleting it. Other visible content after the three content lines is malformed and receives the item-19 repair. The Spaced Repetition plugin owns its schedules, untouched and unaudited.

A missing card has no state to preserve: restore it, unless the entry is a [discipline root](hierarchy.md#establish-discipline-roots), which needs none, from the entry's established main claim, with `??`; it is known fresh for the rest of the run.

## Card set

The [card set](../../wiki-build/references/flashcards-and-emphasis.md#card-set) is one `??` definition card per entry. Routine lint never adds a second card. Any other card, such as a second definition card or a question card from an earlier card set, is a **legacy extra**: its item-19 findings are **report-only**, because it may carry review history or a distinct tested claim. Identify the primary card by the [answer contract](../../wiki-build/references/flashcards-and-emphasis.md#line-3-the-answer), ignoring attachments for the comparison. Never repair or reword a legacy extra; quote each one verbatim, attachments included, in the report. When an extra card's term is a real entity a cited source supports, propose a **split**. Remove or move a card only under an explicitly authorized source-backed refactor that accounts for its tested claim and every attachment, or a request naming that card, or every legacy extra in a scope, for deletion; such a request is sufficient, with no further review gate. A removal advances `updated:` and preserves `read:`.
